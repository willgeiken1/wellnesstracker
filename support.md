# Insight Support

_Last updated: October 6, 2026_

Insight is a personal fitness app built and run by an individual developer as a hobby project. If something isn't working, or you have a question about your data, email **insight.wellnessos@gmail.com**. It's a one-person project, so replies can take a few days.

## Delete your account

- **In the app:** sign in, then go to Settings → Privacy → Delete account and type DELETE to confirm. This permanently removes your sign-in and everything stored with the account, including workouts, food, cardio, measurements, Oura data, and photos, and it erases the copy on that phone. There is no undo.
- **By email:** write to insight.wellnessos@gmail.com from the address you signed up with and ask for your account to be deleted.

If you aren't signed in, that screen offers Erase this phone instead. It removes only what's saved on that phone, not a cloud account.

## FAQ

**Is Insight free?**
Yes. There are no ads and nothing to buy.

**Why won't Snap your plate or Describe it work?**
Both need you to be signed in (Settings → Account). You get 10 food photos and 20 written meal descriptions a day, and both counts reset at midnight in your time zone. Barcode scanning and manual entry don't use either count.

**Where do my meal photos and descriptions go?**
The first time you ask for an AI estimate, Insight asks your permission. If you allow it, the photo and any note you add, or the meal you type, go to Anthropic (Claude) to estimate nutrition. Insight doesn't keep the photo or the text after the estimate comes back. If you tap Cancel, nothing is sent.

**Does Insight work offline?**
Yes. A copy of your data is kept on your device. When you're signed in, it also backs up to your account.

**How do I get a copy of my data?**
Go to Settings → Privacy → Export my data. Insight downloads a zip of CSV files. The progress photo image files aren't in the zip.

**How do I turn off crash reports or usage analytics?**
Go to Settings → Privacy. Send crash reports is on by default, and you can turn it off. Share usage analytics is a separate switch, and it is off unless you turn it on.

**How do I disconnect my Oura Ring?**
Go to Settings → Oura Ring → Disconnect Oura. This removes the Oura login tokens and the Oura data Insight stored. You can also remove Insight's access from your Oura account.
