# Insight Wellness Support

_Last updated: November 20, 2026_

Insight Wellness is built and run by one developer. If something isn't working, or you have a question about your data, email **insight.wellnessos@gmail.com**. Replies can take a few days.

## Delete your account

In the app, go to **Settings › Account** and tap **Delete account**. You can save a backup first. Then type DELETE and tap **Delete permanently**. This removes your sign-in and everything stored with your account, including Oura data and tokens, and erases the copy on that phone. It can't be undone.

If you no longer have the app, email insight.wellnessos@gmail.com from the address you signed up with and ask for your account to be deleted. [Delete your Insight Wellness account](insight-wellness-delete-account) has the details.

## Questions

**Is Insight Wellness free?**
Yes. There are no ads and nothing to buy.

**Where does my Apple Health data go?**
It stays on your iPhone. It is never synced to our servers, never sent to AI services or analytics, and never used for advertising. Insight Wellness only reads from Apple Health and never writes to it. To stop, open the Health app, tap your picture, then Apps, then Insight Wellness, and turn the types off.

**How do I disconnect Oura?**
In Settings, Health & devices. Disconnecting deletes your Oura sign-in tokens and the Oura data Insight Wellness stored.

**Does Insight Wellness work offline?**
Yes. Everything you log is saved on your phone first. When you're signed in, it backs up and syncs once you're back online.

**Is this medical advice?**
No. Estimates, targets and scores are for general fitness only.
