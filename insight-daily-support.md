# Insight Daily Support

_Last updated November 13, 2026_

Insight Daily is built and run by one developer. If something isn't working, or you have a question about your data, email **insight.wellnessos@gmail.com**. Replies can take a few days.

## Delete your account

In the app, go to Settings, Account, Delete account, and type DELETE to confirm. This permanently removes your sign-in and everything stored with the account, including Oura data and tokens, and erases the copy on your iPhone.

If you can't open the app, email insight.wellnessos@gmail.com from the address you signed up with and ask for your account to be deleted. We delete it and confirm by email.

## Questions

**Is Insight Daily free?**
Yes. There are no ads and nothing to buy.

**Do I need an account?**
To try the app, no: choose "Look around first" and it opens with example data that is never saved to an account. To keep your own logs, create a free account. It also backs them up, so they're there when you change phones.

**How do I connect Apple Health?**
During setup, or later in Settings, Health & devices. Insight Daily reads body weight, steps and sleep, and never writes to Apple Health. To stop, open the Health app, tap your picture, then Apps, then Insight Daily, and turn the types off.

**Where does my Apple Health data go?**
Nowhere. It stays on your iPhone. It is never synced, never sent to AI and never used for analytics.

**How do AI food estimates work?**
Sign in, then choose Snap or Describe when you log food. The first time, the app asks your permission. The photo or description you choose to send goes to Anthropic to estimate the nutrition, and is not kept afterwards. There is a daily limit. Barcode scanning and manual entry never use AI. You can withdraw permission in Settings, Privacy.

**Does Insight Daily work offline?**
Yes. Everything is saved on your iPhone first. When you're signed in, it syncs once you're back online.

**What happens when I sign out?**
That account's data is erased from the iPhone. Anything that hasn't synced yet is lost, and the app tells you how many changes that is before you confirm.

**Is this medical advice?**
No. Estimates, targets and scores are for general fitness only.
