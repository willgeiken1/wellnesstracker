# Insight Daily Privacy Policy

_Effective November 13, 2026_

Insight Daily is an iPhone app for logging workouts, food and body measurements and seeing how they fit together. This policy explains what the app handles, where it goes and the choices you have.

## What stays on your iPhone

Everything you log is saved on your iPhone first, and the app works without a connection. Data the app reads from Apple Health always stays on your iPhone: it is never synced to our servers, never sent to AI services, and never used for advertising.

## If you create an account

We collect your email address and a password to sign you in. Your password is handled by our sign-in provider, Supabase, and is never stored in readable form. You must be at least 13, so the app asks for your date of birth when you create an account.

While you are signed in, the app backs up and syncs what you enter in it, so it is available when you sign in again: your profile (name, date of birth, height and units), workouts, sets, routines, plans, goals, cardio, notes, food logs, saved foods, meals and daily targets, weigh-ins and body measurements you typed in, and app settings such as your theme and rest timer. Data read from Apple Health is not included. Your weight goal, sex and activity level stay on your iPhone.

Signing out erases that account's data from the iPhone. Anything that had not finished syncing is lost, and the app warns you first.

## Apple Health

Only if you allow it. Insight Daily reads three things: body weight, steps and sleep. Weigh-ins from Apple Health are saved on your iPhone so they show on Progress; steps and sleep are read when the app needs them and are not saved. None of it is synced, exported, or sent to AI or analytics. Insight Daily never writes to Apple Health. You can turn access off at any time in the Health app: tap your picture, then Apps, then Insight Daily.

## Oura

Only if you connect a ring to your Insight account. With your permission, our server fetches your daily sleep, readiness, activity, heart rate, heart rate variability, temperature and steps from Oura. The Oura sign-in tokens are kept on our server, where the app itself can't read them, and your daily Oura data is stored with your account. You can remove Insight's access in your Oura account at any time.

## AI estimates

Only if you allow it, and only while you are signed in. The first time you ask for one, the app asks your permission and lists exactly what is sent.

- **Food.** When you choose Snap or Describe, the meal photo or the food description you chose to send goes through our server to Anthropic, which estimates the nutrition. A daily limit applies; the app keeps a count of how many you used each day.
- **Workout summaries.** If you ask for one, exercise names, sets, reps and lifted volume go to Anthropic the same way.

Body weight, heart rate, mood, energy, stress, Oura and Apple Health values and your notes are never sent. We don't keep the photo or the text after the estimate comes back. Anthropic handles that content under its own terms and privacy policy. You can withdraw permission at any time in Settings, Privacy.

## Barcode lookups

When you scan or type a barcode, only the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts.

## Who handles your data

Supabase stores accounts and synced data in the United States (Ohio) and runs the server functions above. Anthropic receives only what you choose to send for an AI estimate. Open Food Facts receives barcode numbers. Oura supplies your ring data when you connect it.

## What we don't do

We don't sell your data, show ads, use it for advertising or track you across other apps and websites. The app has no analytics or crash-reporting service. We don't share your data with other users.

## Your choices

You can edit or delete anything you log, turn Apple Health access off, withdraw permission for AI estimates, and sign out. To delete your account and everything stored with it, including Oura data and tokens, email us from the address you signed up with (below); we delete it and confirm by email.

## How long we keep data

Until you delete it or your account is deleted.

## Children

Insight Daily is not for anyone under 13.

## Not medical advice

Estimates, targets and scores are for general fitness. They are not medical advice and not a substitute for a doctor.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and tell you in the app when the change matters.
