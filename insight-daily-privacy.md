# Insight Daily Privacy Policy

_Effective November 12, 2026_

Insight Daily is an iPhone app for logging workouts, food, body measurements and recovery. This policy explains what the app handles, where it goes and the choices you have.

## What stays on your iPhone

Everything you log is saved on your iPhone first. Data Insight Daily reads from Apple Health always stays on your iPhone. It is never synced to our servers, never sent to AI services or analytics, and never used for advertising.

## If you create an account

We collect your email address and password to sign you in. Your password is handled by our sign-in provider and is never stored in readable form. While you are signed in, the app backs up and syncs your profile (name, date of birth, height and units), workouts, sets, routines, cardio, plans, goals, notes, food logs, saved foods, targets, weigh-ins and body measurements that you entered in the app, and the details of your progress photos, so they are available on your other devices. Health data read from Apple Health is not included.

## Apple Health

Only if you allow it. Insight Daily reads your steps, active calories, exercise minutes, workouts, heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, sleep stages and time asleep, weight, body fat, VO2 max, water and mindful minutes to show your trends and daily Score. It does not write to Apple Health. You can change this at any time in the Health app or in iPhone Settings.

## Oura

Only if you connect your ring. With your permission, Insight Daily reads your daily sleep, readiness, activity, heart rate, heart rate variability, temperature and workouts from Oura. The sign-in tokens are kept on our server, where the app itself can't read them, so the server can fetch your data, and your daily Oura data is stored with your account.

## Barcode lookups

When you scan or type a barcode, only the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts.

## Crash reports

While Send crash reports is on, which it is by default, crash details and basic device information are sent to Sentry, along with your account ID if you're signed in. They don't include your logs, health data, photos or email. You can turn this off in Settings.

## Usage analytics

Only if you turn on Share usage analytics. A short list of feature-use events, such as which tab you opened or that a workout was saved, is sent to PostHog with your account ID. They don't include health values, food text, notes, photos or email. You can turn this off at any time.

## Who handles your data

Supabase stores accounts and synced data in the United States (Ohio). Open Food Facts handles barcode lookups. Sentry receives crash reports and PostHog receives usage analytics. Oura supplies your ring data when you connect it.

## What we don't do

We don't sell your data, show ads, use it for advertising or track you across other apps and websites. We don't share your data with other users.

## Your choices

You can edit or delete anything you log, delete a date range, export your data, and remove Apple Health access. You can disconnect Oura in Settings, which deletes their sign-in tokens and the data Insight Daily stored from them; you can also remove Insight Daily's access in your Oura account. You can delete your account in Settings, which permanently removes your account and everything stored with it, including Oura data and tokens. Signing out leaves the copy on your iPhone until you erase it.

## How long we keep data

Until you delete it or delete your account.

## Children

Insight Daily is not for anyone under 13, and asks for a date of birth when you create an account.

## Not medical advice

Estimates, targets and scores are for general fitness. They are not medical advice and not a substitute for a doctor.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and tell you in the app when the change matters.
