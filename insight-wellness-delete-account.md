# Delete your Insight Wellness account

_Last updated: November 16, 2026_

You can delete your Insight Wellness account and its data at any time, from the app or by email.

## In the app

1. Open Insight Wellness and sign in.
2. Go to **Settings › Account** and tap **Delete account**.
3. You can save a backup first. Then type DELETE and tap **Delete permanently**.

## By email

If you no longer have the app, email insight.wellnessos@gmail.com from the address you signed up with and ask for your account to be deleted. We delete it and reply when it's done.

## What is deleted

Your sign-in and everything stored with your account: your profile, workouts, sets, routines, cardio, plans, goals, notes, food logs, saved foods, targets, weigh-ins, body measurements, progress photo details, and your Oura sign-in tokens and Oura data. Nothing is kept after deletion, and it can't be undone.

Deleting your account in the app also erases the copy on that phone. A copy on another phone stays there until you erase it in that phone's Settings.

See the [privacy policy](insight-daily-privacy) for more.
