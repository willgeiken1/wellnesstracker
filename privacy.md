This application is a personal project that accesses Oura Ring data (sleep, readiness, activity, heart rate, and workouts) only for the account holder who authorizes it. Data is used solely to display trends and insights to that user. Data is stored locally on the user's own device and is not sold, shared, or disclosed to any third party. Users can revoke access at any time through their Oura account settings. Contact: willgeiken1@icloud.com
